### Your Brain Has Two Systems

**System 1**: Fast, automatic, unconscious
- Runs on autopilot
- Makes instant decisions
- Where all your habits live
- Extremely energy-efficient

**System 2**: Slow, deliberate, conscious
- Requires effort
- Makes rational decisions
- Where willpower lives
- Burns a lot of mental energy

**Here's the problem**: Checking your phone is a System 1 habit.

You don't consciously think "I shall now open Instagram to endlessly scroll through stories." You just... do it.

Your hand reaches for your phone. Your thumb finds the Instagram icon. You're scrolling before your conscious mind even catches up.

**System 1 always wins** when it's competing with System 2. Why? Because it's faster and requires zero willpower.

### The Only Thing That Works

You can't fight System 1 with System 2. You'll lose every time.

But you know what *does* work?

**Interrupting System 1 just long enough for System 2 to wake up.**

That's what speed bumps do. They don't prevent you from driving. They force you to slow down and pay attention.

That's what Intentional Friction does. We interrupt the autopilot habit *before* it takes over.

3 seconds. That's all it takes.

In those 3 seconds, System 2 wakes up and asks: **"Wait, why am I doing this?"**

And suddenly, you have a choice.
