# Privacy Policy for Intentional Friction

**Last Updated:** November 2025

## Overview

Intentional Friction ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## Our Privacy-First Approach

**We collect ZERO personal information. Everything stays on your device.**

### What We DON'T Collect

- ❌ No personal identification information (name, email, phone)
- ❌ No location data
- ❌ No usage analytics sent to servers
- ❌ No advertising IDs or tracking pixels
- ❌ No data sold to third parties
- ❌ No cloud syncing or remote storage
- ❌ No user accounts required

### What We DO Collect (Locally Only)

All data is stored locally on your device using Hive (local database):

1. **Friction Moments**
   - Timestamp of friction moment
   - App name you were trying to open
   - Friction mode shown (mirror, question, etc.)
   - Your choice (proceed or close)
   - Optional emotional state (if you choose to track it)

2. **App Settings**
   - Onboarding completion status
   - Friction enabled/disabled
   - Friction intensity preference
   - List of monitored apps

3. **Pattern Analysis**
   - Weekly insights generated locally
   - Zombie hour detection
   - All calculations done on-device

### Data Storage

- **Location:** All data is stored in your device's local storage
- **Encryption:** Data is protected by your device's encryption
- **Backups:** Included in device backups (iCloud/Google Drive)
- **Deletion:** Uninstalling the app deletes all data permanently

### Permissions We Request

#### Android
- **Usage Access Permission:** Required to detect when you open monitored apps
  - Only used locally to trigger friction moments
  - Never sent to servers
  - Can be revoked in Android settings

#### iOS
- **No permissions required:** iOS uses manual triggers or Shortcuts (user-initiated)

### Third-Party Services

We use NO third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app is completely standalone.

### Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. All data stays on the device.

### Data Export

You can export your friction moments at any time:
- Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data
- Exports as JSON file you control
- No data leaves your device except via your explicit action

### Data Deletion

Delete your data anytime:
- Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Data
- Uninstall the app (removes all data)
- Clear app data in device settings

### Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect app updates. Changes will be posted in-app and on our website.

### Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy laws:
- **Right to Access:** Export your data anytime
- **Right to Delete:** Delete all data instantly
- **Right to Portability:** Export data in standard format
- **Right to Object:** Stop data collection by disabling features

Since all data is local, you have complete control.

### International Users

Data never leaves your device, regardless of where you are located. No international transfers occur.
