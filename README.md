# Intentional Friction

**A Digital Wellbeing App That Creates Self-Awareness Through Thoughtful Pauses**

---

## What Is This?

Intentional Friction is not another screen time tracker or app blocker. It's a tool that helps you understand *why* you reach for your phone and makes you consciously choose whether to proceed.

**The Core Idea**: The problem isn't screen time—it's unconscious screen time. We don't need more barriers; we need more awareness.

---

## How It Works

```
You reach for Instagram
       ↓
[FRICTION MOMENT]
"You've opened Instagram 12 times today.
What are you hoping to find right now?"
       ↓
You pause... reflect... then consciously choose
       ↓
Over time, the pause becomes internalized
```

---

## Why This Matters

**Research shows**:
- We check our phones **142 times per day** (mostly unconsciously)
- **4.2 hours daily** on social media
- "Dopamine-scrolling" uses the same addictive mechanisms as slot machines
- BUT: Digital detoxes and app blockers have 90%+ failure rates

**The missing piece**: A moment of reflection *before* mindless consumption, not guilt *after*.

---

## Key Features

### 🪞 **Five Friction Modes**
1. **The Mirror**: Shows your usage patterns
2. **The Question**: Surfaces underlying needs
3. **The Trade-off**: Makes opportunity costs visible
4. **The Breath**: Pure pause, just breathe
5. **The Alternative**: Suggests replacement activities

### 🧠 **Adaptive Intelligence**
- Learns when you're being mindless vs. intentional
- Adjusts friction based on your patterns
- Different modes for different emotional triggers
- Respects context (doesn't interrupt urgent use)

### 📊 **Insight Dashboard**
- See when/why you reach for your phone
- Track mindfulness rate over time
- Weekly AI-generated insights
- Pattern recognition across days/weeks

### 🔒 **Privacy-First**
- All data stays on your device
- No tracking, no ads, no data sales
- Optional encrypted cloud sync
- One-tap data export and deletion

## The Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Awareness Over Restriction**: We don't block; we pause
2. **Friction as Mirror**: Discomfort is data, not punishment
3. **Respect for Context**: Not all screen time is equal
4. **Honest Trade-offs**: Sometimes scrolling IS the right choice
5. **Beautiful Difficulty**: Friction should feel meaningful

### What Makes This Different?

**Not Screen Time Tracking**: Shows *why* you reach, not just *what* happened

**Not App Blocking**: Says "let's think about this," not "no"

**Not Gamified**: No streaks, no shame—success is awareness, not abstinence

**Not One-Size-Fits-All**: Learns YOUR patterns and adapts to YOUR life
